## Research Methodology

Each competitor was evaluated across 25 dimensions:

| Category | Dimensions |
|----------|------------|
| **Surface** | Homepage, Hero, Offer, Pricing, Typography, Layout, Visual hierarchy |
| **Conversion** | CTA placement, Trust building, Lead generation, Funnels, Forms, Checkout |
| **Content** | Before & after, Testimonials, Copywriting, Brand voice, SEO |
| **Visual** | Colour palette, Photography, Illustration, Animations |
| **UX** | Mobile UX, Desktop UX, Loading speed, Accessibility |
| **Strategy** | Psychology, Strengths, Weaknesses |

**Premium rating (1–5):** 1 = mass freemium · 3 = mid-market · 5 = ultra-premium/concierge/medical

## Cross-Market Pattern Analysis

### What Premium Winners Do

| Pattern | Examples | LekkiStart application |
|---------|----------|------------------------|
| Price visible early | Future ($199/mo hero), Born Fitness ($249/mo), Forge PT (tier table) | Three pricing cards above fold |
| Quiz/intake as brand | Noom, Hims & Hers, Calibrate | Application form as editorial journey |
| Science + human | Caliber, Calibrate, Precision Nutrition | Evidence-based copy + named coach |
| Editorial not gym | Oura, Function Health, Superpower | No dumbbells, no neon, no shred |
| Outcome data with caveats | Calibrate (19% at 36mo), WW Clinic (21% at 12mo) | Honest timelines, no "lose 10kg in 2 weeks" |
| App as delivery, web as conversion | Future, Noom, Respo | Marketing site ≠ app; site sells coaching |
| Habit pillars not macros | Calibrate (4 pillars), Fern (Nourish/Move/Feel) | Training + nutrition + accountability + education |
| Limited roster scarcity | Born Fitness, DietaGLP VIP (max 10 clients) | Premium tier capacity limit |
| Money-back guarantee | Future (30 days), Caliber (30 days) | 14–30 day satisfaction guarantee |

### What Mass Market Does (Avoid)

| Anti-pattern | Examples | Why it fails for premium obesity coaching |
|--------------|----------|------------------------------------------|
| Celebrity/influencer hero | Diet & Training by Ann, Fit Lovers, BeDiet | Signals diet fad, not medical-grade support |
| Bodybuilding positioning | Łukasz Piłat, RP Strength, many PL trainers | Alienates obese audience |
| Hidden pricing | Respo ("Sprawdź ofertę"), many PL dietetycy | Friction at decision moment |
| Before/after grids | Cox Coaching, most PL Instagram coaches | Harmful, stigmatizing, legally risky |
| App-only onboarding | BetterMe, MyFitnessPal | No human relationship established |
| GLP-1 as entire brand | Ro, Hims, Eden | Medical Rx ≠ lifestyle coaching (adjacent, not core) |
| Generic Tailwind SaaS layout | 80% of fitness startups | Zero brand recognition |

### Typography Landscape

| Tier | Common fonts | LekkiStart must avoid |
|------|--------------|----------------------|
| Mass fitness | Poppins, Montserrat, Inter | All banned list fonts |
| Premium health | Söhne, GT America, Tiempos, Editorial New | — use these families |
| Clinical telehealth | System fonts, Inter, Circular | Cold, generic |
| Polish mid-market | Open Sans, Lato, Roboto | Generic |

### Colour Landscape

| Segment | Dominant palettes |
|---------|-------------------|
| Fitness influencer | Neon green on black, orange gradients, electric blue |
| Medical weight loss | White + teal + soft blue (clinical) |
| Premium dark health | `#0A0A0A` + warm off-white + single amber/sage accent |
| Polish diet apps | Pink, mint, bright yellow (friendly/childish) |

**LekkiStart:** Dark canvas + warm parchment text + single amber accent (`#C4A574`).

## Deep-Dive: Tier-1 Competitors (30)

### 1. Future (future.co) — Premium Async Coaching Gold Standard

| Dimension | Analysis |
|-----------|----------|
| **Hero** | "Personal training, reimagined" — benefit-led, not feature-led. Price in hero: "$50 first month, then $199/month" |
| **Offer** | Dedicated 1:1 coach, daily accountability, personalized plan, Apple Watch integration |
| **Pricing** | Fully transparent: $199/mo, prepaid discounts ($149/mo annual). 30-day refund |
| **Typography** | Clean grotesque, large headlines, generous whitespace |
| **Layout** | Full-width sections, alternating content/imagery, sticky CTA on mobile |
| **CTA** | "Find Your Coach" — action-oriented, repeated 4–6x |
| **Trust** | 4.9 App Store, member results, coach credentials |
| **Psychology** | Accountability partner framing; removes "train alone" pain |
| **Funnel** | 3-min quiz → coach match → trial |
| **Photography** | Real athletes, editorial quality, not stock gym |
| **Weakness** | Fitness-first (not obesity-specific); requires Apple Watch; US-centric |
| **LekkiStart lesson** | Price in hero + guarantee + quiz onboarding |
